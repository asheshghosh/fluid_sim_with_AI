Research contract

Exact mode is the trusted finite-difference heat equation. AI mode is an in-browser neural-operator-style macro surrogate that advances many solver-equivalent steps per call. Hybrid mode periodically replaces the surrogate with exact physics.

Treat high error or hotspot drift as a scientific signal: it means the surrogate needs training data, a smaller stride, or more frequent correction for that geometry.
